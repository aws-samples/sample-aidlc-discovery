Recovery card

What goes wrong on the day, by phase, and what to say or do. From run-sheet.md "When things go wrong" and the Common Failures in each skill.

PhaseSymptomSay or do
InstallNot installed, or behaves like an older version"List my skills", then the module check. Delete the skill in Customize, Skills (the ⋮ menu), import again with Create, From folder, and start a new chat. Never copy files into the profile by hand.
AnySkips steps or jumps to verdicts"Stop. Follow the aidlc-discovery skill one step at a time. Load the module for this phase and start at Step 1."
AnyChat lost, or skill reinstalled mid-workshopNew chat with folder access: "Continue the AI-DLC Discovery workshop for [project] from discovery/[project]/PROJECT.md. [What is done.] Start at [the next step]."
AnyA module path doesn't resolveTell it to retry with the skill's full installed path.
SpaceSomeone else takes over facilitationIn their own AWS Quick, with the Space attached: "Continue the AI-DLC Discovery workshop for [project] from discovery/[project]/PROJECT.md in the [space] space. I'm taking over as [role]. Start at [step]." The previous driver stops writing.
SpaceTwo people wrote at onceThe last save wins. Ask both what they changed, re-read from the Space, and redo the lost change as the agreed driver.
SpaceSaves fail or land in a local folderThe Space isn't attached: the + menu, then Spaces; then save again.
SpaceA Viewer tries to driveShare the Space with them as Owner, or let an Owner drive.
SpaceReal customer data in the team SpaceOnly if nobody who shouldn't see it has access, confirmed and recorded in PROJECT.md. Otherwise keep it local or in a separate restricted Space.
SetupSponsor leaves after kickoffBefore they go: "Is this the metric you care about?"
SetupNo baseline for the business metricName who gets it by the 7-day follow-up.
SignalsData doesn't match the productIt must stop and offer real data, Signal Briefs, or a dataset prepared beforehand. Never accept data written on the spot.
Signals"Skip the hypotheses, just show the data"Hold the line: hypotheses first, or the room can't check itself.
SignalsData contains personal informationKeep it out of saved artifacts; tell the team; suggest masking before the next run.
SignalsThe room accepts everythingAsk the person whose belief was contradicted to argue their case. "What would make this fail?" before every checkpoint.
PersonasTwo personas are the same personMerge them; set-asides go to set-aside-personas.md.
ScoringTeam brings features"What problem does this solve, and for whom?"
ScoringEvery candidate scores 3 to 4Candidates are too broad; make them more specific.
ScoringWeight change requested after scoringDecline: weights are fixed before the first score. Record the request.
ScoringThe team overrides everythingFine; ask what evidence each rests on, and label Assumed where there is none.
ScoringThe assistant questions an overrideThe person who made it has the last word: "Does that change your view, or do we keep it?"
ScoringA favourite fails the gateRecord the reason. If someone wants it alive, an open hypothesis with a cheap test.
PR/FAQReads like a vision document"Rewrite the press release as exactly seven paragraphs, one job each, 500 to 700 words, and run the writing audit."
PR/FAQDescribes technologyRewrite the first paragraph from the customer's side.
PR/FAQAccepted without changesRead it aloud; stop where someone winces: "How would we say it?"
ReadinessREDTen minutes on the gaps before the PRD. AMBER: carry gaps with owners; don't chase GREEN.
RiskiestEvery assumption looks risky"Which one, if wrong, means nobody uses it?" Everyone chooses before anyone speaks.
PrototypeOne plain page"Load modules/discovery-prototype/references/prototype-shell.md and copy that HTML exactly, filling only the edit region." Ask it to quote the EDIT BELOW line first.
PrototypeMobile and Desktop tabs dead in the session tabRebuild from the current shell, or open the file from the working folder in a browser.
PrototypeCompare page shows empty frames in AWS QuickOpen prototype/compare.html from the working folder in a browser.
PrototypeThe assistant asks a factual questionGet the answer from the person who knows, or mark it Needs evidence. A guessed fact becomes a promise in the brief.
PrototypeLooks finished but tests nothingGo back to the test moment.
PrototypeIterating on colours"What would the persona trip over?"
PrototypeThe assistant changed the agreed planRead each change; ask its owner "Keep it, or change it back?"
PrototypeA fix promises something unconfirmedSay what it depends on and who confirms; if kept, log Needs evidence.
ValidateTeam wants to skip ValidateOffer the 20-minute version: four risks and the decision.
ValidateEvery risk supportedAsk what the satisfied persona loses, and what the top pre-mortem reason says.
ValidateVanity success criteriaTie each to the business metric or adoption, with a number and a date.
ValidateCommit with the riskiest assumption open"Commit to building now, or to testing first?" Record conditions, owners, dates.
ValidateThe team can't agreeRecord each position and its evidence; ask the sponsor at the readout.
HandoffA required artifact is missingList it with the skill that produces it; never write a partial brief.
HandoffThe brief carries things the assistant drafted for the teamBefore the brief, confirm, change, or label as a proposal: the reason, interim targets, kill criteria, owners.
HandoffBrief is too longSummarise each section to essentials and link to artifacts.
TimeRunning outProtect Validate and Handoff. "Agree with all" in scoring, skip optional research, one prototype iteration.
