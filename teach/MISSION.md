# Mission: The AI-DLC Discovery method, stage by stage

## Why
Train new facilitators to run the AI-DLC Discovery workshop, and build presentations about it, from one clear account of every stage: what it asks, what it does, what it leaves behind, and how the room goes wrong.

## Success looks like
- Explain each phase (Setup, Signals, Working Backwards, Prototype, Validate, Handoff) without notes: its question, its skill, its inputs, its artifacts, its checkpoint.
- Trace the relay: which file each phase needs before it can start, and why.
- Name the facilitator move and the common failure for every checkpoint.
- Turn `reference/phase-map.html` straight into slides: one line and one artifact per phase.

## Constraints
- The user wrote the method and the skills; teach the structure for others, not the basics of product discovery.
- Ground every claim in this repo (`skills/`, `facilitator/`) or a cited outside source.
- Lessons are short, with a quiz for retrieval. Reference sheets must print well.

## Out of scope
- Installing or debugging Amazon Quick.
- Changing the skills themselves.
- The AI-DLC build lifecycle after Handoff.
