# Signal Brief: employers ask for telehealth
Brief ID: SB-07 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: HR account team

**Theme:** Some employers ask whether the plan will add a telehealth service.

**Job or desired outcome:** Offer employees a quick way to see a doctor without leaving work.

**Evidence:**
- "Our competitor's plan includes virtual visits. Can yours?" (HR director, renewal meeting)
- "It would be nice for employees to have telehealth." (HR manager, quarterly review)
- 43 of 180 employer groups reviewed asked about telehealth. 116 broker escalations (9%) passed on the same request
- Among members, telehealth was 2% of calls, and "telehealth" was searched 3,140 times in the period

**Estimated impact:** Employers say it would help renewals; we have no evidence it moved any renewal (estimate).

**Confidence:** Low, because the request comes from employers and brokers, not from members, and member signals are small.

**Segment:** HR teams at employer groups comparing plans at renewal.

**Suggested next step:** Ask members who used a virtual visit elsewhere what they used it for, before deciding anything.
