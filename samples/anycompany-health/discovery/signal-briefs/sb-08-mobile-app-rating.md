# Signal Brief: the app has a poor rating
Brief ID: SB-08 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: broker team

**Theme:** The member app has a low store rating, and some employers ask for a better app.

**Job or desired outcome:** Handle health plan tasks on the phone.

**Evidence:**
- "Your app is 2.9 stars. Our employees notice." (HR director, passed on by the broker)
- "Rebuild the app and half these calls go away." (broker, in a quarterly review)
- App store rating 2.9 from 1,860 reviews. In 54% of the 1 and 2-star reviews, the complaint is about claim status, not about the app itself
- 34 of 180 employer groups reviewed asked for a better app

**Estimated impact:** A low rating may hurt how employers see the plan at renewal (estimate).

**Confidence:** Medium, because the rating is solid, but the reviews point at claim information more than at the app.

**Segment:** Members who use the app, and the employers who hear about it.

**Suggested next step:** Read 100 low-rated reviews and sort them by what is actually missing.
