# Signal Brief: some employers want a wellness rewards program
Brief ID: SB-09 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: HR account team

**Theme:** A few employers ask for a wellness rewards program with points and challenges.

**Job or desired outcome:** Encourage employees to stay healthy and engaged with the plan.

**Evidence:**
- "A step challenge with rewards would be a nice extra for our people." (HR manager, quarterly review)
- "Engagement is our theme this year. Could the plan help?" (HR director, renewal meeting)
- 24 of 180 employer groups reviewed mentioned it. "wellness rewards" was searched 930 times in the period, and no member services contact reason covers it

**Estimated impact:** None measured. Employers describe it as a nice extra.

**Confidence:** Low, because the only source is a minority of employers, and members show almost no interest.

**Segment:** HR teams running employee engagement programs.

**Suggested next step:** Ask the employers who raised it whether they would choose a plan because of it.
