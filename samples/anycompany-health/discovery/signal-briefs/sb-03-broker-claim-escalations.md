# Signal Brief: brokers chase claims for their clients' employees
Brief ID: SB-03 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: broker team

**Theme:** Brokers spend their time chasing claim status for the employees of the companies they serve.

**Job or desired outcome:** Answer a client's question about a claim without opening an escalation.

**Evidence:**
- "Half my week is chasing claims for my clients' staff. I can't see anything either, so I email you." (broker, in a quarterly review)
- "My client's CFO asked why an employee's surgery claim has been pending for five weeks. I had no answer." (broker escalation)
- 498 of 1,240 broker escalations in the period were about claim delays or status (40%), the largest category

**Estimated impact:** Brokers say slow claim answers come up in renewal conversations; we have no measure of lost renewals (estimate).

**Confidence:** Medium, because the escalation counts are solid, but they show what brokers chase, not what members experience.

**Segment:** Brokers serving companies with 50 to 1,000 employees.

**Suggested next step:** Ask five brokers which claim information they would need to answer a client without escalating.
