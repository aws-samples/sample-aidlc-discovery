# Signal Brief: the provider directory sends members to the wrong place
Brief ID: SB-05 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: member services team

**Theme:** Members call because the provider directory shows doctors who are not in network, have moved, or are not taking new patients.

**Job or desired outcome:** Find an in-network doctor who will actually see them.

**Evidence:**
- "Three of the five dentists the site listed said they don't take our plan anymore." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- "The phone number for the clinic was a pizza place." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- 12,005 member services calls in the period were about the provider directory (14%)
- 31,290 portal searches for "find a doctor"; 27% were followed by a call within 48 hours

**Estimated impact:** Wasted appointments for members, and some out-of-network bills that later become complaints (estimate).

**Confidence:** High, because the call volume and the search follow-up rate agree, and brokers report the same problem.

**Segment:** Members looking for a new doctor or dentist, often after moving or a change of plan.

**Suggested next step:** Sample 100 directory listings and call them, to measure how many are wrong.
