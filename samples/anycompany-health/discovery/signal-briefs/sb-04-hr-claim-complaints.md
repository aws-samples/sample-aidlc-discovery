# Signal Brief: employees complain to HR about claims
Brief ID: SB-04 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: HR account team

**Theme:** Employer HR teams field employees' complaints about claims that seem stuck, and pass them on to us.

**Job or desired outcome:** Keep employees happy with their health plan without HR becoming a claims help desk.

**Evidence:**
- "Our people come to HR because they can't get a straight answer about their claims." (HR director, renewal meeting)
- "We like the plan. We don't like being the middleman for claim questions." (HR manager, quarterly review)
- 62 of 180 employer groups reviewed in the period raised employees' claim complaints, the most common theme

**Estimated impact:** Claim complaints come up in renewal meetings, and account managers believe they weaken renewals (estimate, not measured).

**Confidence:** Medium, because it is a consistent theme across employers, but we hear it second hand through HR.

**Segment:** HR teams at employer groups with more than 200 employees.

**Suggested next step:** Compare renewal outcomes for employers who raised claim complaints with those who did not.
