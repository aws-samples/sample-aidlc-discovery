# Signal Brief: members cannot see where their claim is
Brief ID: SB-01 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: member services team

**Theme:** Members call to ask where their claim is, because the portal shows only "received" or "processed".

**Job or desired outcome:** Know what is happening with a claim, and when it will be paid, without calling.

**Evidence:**
- "I've called three times this month. Every time I'm told it's in process. In process where?" (member call, summarized by the agent)
- "The portal just says received. Received three weeks ago. That tells me nothing." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- "I don't want to call. I want to look it up like a parcel." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- 34,334 of 85,519 member services calls in the period were about claim status (40%), about 120 calls for every 1,000 claims

**Estimated impact:** About 6,243 calls a month. At an estimated 6 minutes a call, about 624 agent hours a month (estimate).

**Confidence:** High, because the pattern is the largest contact reason every month, and brokers and employer HR teams report the same thing.

**Segment:** Members in employer group plans who file claims often, mostly families and people with ongoing treatment.

**Suggested next step:** Check whether the claims system can show a member the real status of each claim and the expected decision date.
