# Signal Brief: members do not know what we need from them
Brief ID: SB-02 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: member services team

**Theme:** When a claim is waiting for information, members do not know what is missing, so they call or send the wrong document.

**Job or desired outcome:** Send the right document once, and get the claim paid.

**Evidence:**
- "The letter says additional information required. What information? From me or from my doctor?" (member call, summarized by the agent)
- "I uploaded the receipt twice. Then they told me they needed the referral, not the receipt." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- "I only found out it was stuck when the dentist sent me the bill." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- 28,052 of 286,842 claims in the period waited for information from the member (10%). Agents estimate about half of claim-status calls are about one of these

**Estimated impact:** Pended claims take about two weeks longer to decide, and many generate two or three calls each (estimate).

**Confidence:** High, because the pended volume comes from the claims system and agents see the same questions daily.

**Segment:** Members with claims that need a referral, a receipt, or information from their provider.

**Suggested next step:** List the five most common reasons a claim is pended, and check whether the claims system records which one applies.
