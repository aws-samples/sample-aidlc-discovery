# Signal Brief: directory errors turn into surprise bills
Brief ID: SB-06 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: broker team

**Theme:** Employees see a provider the directory listed as in network, then receive an out-of-network bill.

**Job or desired outcome:** Trust that a listed provider is covered.

**Evidence:**
- "My client's employee checked the directory, saw the specialist, and got a bill for the full amount." (broker escalation)
- "Every surprise bill turns into a complaint to the employer, then to me." (broker, in a quarterly review)
- 206 broker escalations in the period were about directory errors or surprise bills (17%)

**Estimated impact:** Each case usually ends in a complaint, and some in a refund or an exception payment (estimate).

**Confidence:** Medium, because escalations are solid but we do not know how many members pay a surprise bill without escalating.

**Segment:** Employees of client companies who see specialists.

**Suggested next step:** Count the exception payments made for directory errors in the period.
