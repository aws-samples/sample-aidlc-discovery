# Signal Brief: the digital ID card works
Brief ID: SB-10 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-05-01 to 2026-10-15 | Author: member services team

**Theme:** Members find and use the digital member ID card without help.

**Job or desired outcome:** Show proof of coverage at a pharmacy or a doctor's office.

**Evidence:**
- "Pulled it up on my phone at the pharmacy. Easy." (member feedback, post-call survey)
- "The only part of the app I never have trouble with is the ID card." (member call, summarized by the agent)
- Only 3% of member services calls were about the ID card. "id card" was searched 26,570 times, and only 3% of those searches were followed by a call within 48 hours
- 16 of 180 employer groups reviewed praised it without being asked

**Estimated impact:** Positive: calls about ID cards dropped after the digital card launched (estimate; the before figure is not in this data).

**Confidence:** High, because calls, searches, and employer comments agree.

**Segment:** All members.

**Suggested next step:** Keep it as it is, and use it as the pattern for how members want to find information.
