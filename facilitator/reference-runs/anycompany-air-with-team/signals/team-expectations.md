# Team expectations: AnyCompany Air website and app
Written 2026-10-29, before the signal analysis. Do not edit after the analysis runs.

## Q1. Biggest customer problem today (in the team's words)
- **Head of digital:** "The booking flow. Our website looks dated next to the competition, and booking is where we make money."
- **Commercial director:** "Price. Customers complain about fares."
- **UX designer:** "Choosing a seat. People can't see what they're paying for, so upgrades don't sell. A 3D seat map would help."

## Q2. Who has it most (in the team's words)
- **Head of digital:** "Leisure travellers booking on the website."
- **Head of corporate accounts:** "Corporate travellers. They're a third of revenue and their travel managers complain to me every week."

## Q3. Why the team thinks so (in the team's words)
- **Head of digital:** "Reviews mention the booking flow, and the competition's sites look better."
- **Customer service operations lead:** "Every summer the call queues explode. We need more agents from June to September."
- **Head of corporate accounts:** "Travel managers tell me in every account review."

## Q4. What else the team expects the feedback to show (in the team's words)
- **Engineering lead:** "Nothing big. The app's ratings for booking and check-in are good. I'd be careful with anything that changes bookings after they're made."
- **Customer service operations lead:** "Lost and delayed bags. That's the biggest call reason."

## Expectations
1. We expect leisure travellers booking on the website to complain about a dated booking flow, because reviews mention the booking flow and the competition's sites look better. (Head of digital)
2. We expect customers to complain mainly about fares, because the commercial director hears price complaints. (Commercial director)
3. We expect customers choosing a seat to say they can't see what they're paying for, so seat upgrades don't sell, because the UX designer saw a competitor's 3D cabin view. (UX designer)
4. We expect corporate travellers to have the most problems, because travel managers raise them in every account review. (Head of corporate accounts; the specific problem was not named)
5. We expect call volumes to spike every summer (June to September) beyond what the call centre can handle, and that more agents is the answer, because the queues explode every summer. (Customer service operations lead)
6. We expect the feedback to show no major problem with the app's booking and check-in, because their app ratings are good. (Engineering lead)
7. We expect lost and delayed bags to be the biggest reason customers call, because that is what the call centre sees. (Customer service operations lead)

## Caution recorded (not testable by the data)
- **Engineering lead:** "I'd be careful with anything that changes bookings after they're made." Carried forward to the risk review.

## Q5. What would surprise us (in the team's words)
- **Head of digital:** "If booking turned out not to matter."
- **Engineering lead:** "If customers wanted to change their own bookings in the app."

## What would surprise us
- That booking does not matter to customers' problems. (Head of digital)
- That customers want to change their own bookings in the app. (Engineering lead)
