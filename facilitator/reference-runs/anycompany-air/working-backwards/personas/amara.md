# Amara Okafor
**Tagline:** The Stranded Family Planner — when a flight is cancelled, she needs a new one that keeps everyone together and gets them there in time.
**Based on:** 43 feedback items (leisure disruption records; plus family and group items CALL-0020, REV-0069, AGY-0021, AGY-0025)
**Confidence:** HIGH
**Role:** End user

## Identity
- **Age:** 35-45
- **Location:** Mid-size European city, flies from a regional airport
- **Occupation:** Pharmacist, works shifts
- **Bio:** Flies AnyCompany Air two or three times a year with her partner and two children, usually to visit family or on holiday. Books directly in the app. Plans every leg months ahead, so a cancellation breaks a chain of hotel, train, and work plans.

## Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal:** Get the whole family to the destination on the same flight, on a day that still works.
- **Secondary Goals:** Keep the hotel and onward train; avoid losing a day of leave; know what she is entitled to (refund, hotel).
- **Definition of Success:** A new flight she chose, for all four of them, confirmed in the app before she leaves the gate.
- **Deeper Motivation:** Being the one who keeps the family calm and in control when plans fall apart.

## Pain Points
- **Challenges:** The automatic rebooking picks a time or day that does not work or splits the family; the app shows alternatives she cannot pick; hold times of 60 to 175 minutes; the hotline closes overnight.
- **Blockers:** No Choose action on the alternatives screen; vouchers and refunds only by phone.
- **Workarounds:** Calls immediately and stays on hold while the children wait; queues at the desk; books her own hotel.
- **Emotional Impact:** Helpless and angry: "on your own".

## Behavior
- **Current Solutions:** App for booking and check-in, phone when anything goes wrong.
- **Tools:** Airline app, phone, maps for trains.
- **Frequency:** As needed (a few trips a year)
- **Tech Affinity:** Medium
- **Decision Style:** Gut feeling under time pressure

## Context & Environment
- **Usage Context:** At the gate or in the terminal, minutes after a cancellation alert, with children in tow.
- **Devices:** Android phone
- **Time Pressure:** High: the good alternatives fill up while she waits.
- **Influencers:** Her partner; reviews from other parents.

## Quotes

> "Spent the evening on hold instead of with my family." — NPS verbatim, cancelled flight, score 3
> "The delay wasn't the problem. Not being able to change my flight myself was." — NPS verbatim, delayed flight, score 4

## Scenario
**Cancelled at the gate, two children, one evening**
The 17:25 home is cancelled at the gate. The app sends an alert and shows three other flights, one tonight via another city with four seats left. She cannot pick it; the airline has already moved them to a flight in two days. She calls, waits 90 minutes, and the evening flight is full by the time she gets through. What she wants: tap the evening flight, see all four names on it, done.

## Communication Style
- **Tone:** Direct, practical
- **Speech Patterns:** Talks about "us" and "the kids"; concrete times and days
- **Frustration Signals:** Counts minutes on hold; one-star reviews that praise the crew and blame the process
- **Enthusiasm Signals:** "Smooth", "easy", recommending the airline to friends
