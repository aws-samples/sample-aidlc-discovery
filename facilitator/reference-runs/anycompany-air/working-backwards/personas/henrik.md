# Henrik Sørensen
**Tagline:** The Accountable Travel Manager — chooses the airline for 3,000 travellers and renews on how it handles the bad days.
**Based on:** 12 corporate account notes on disruption and 10 travel agency escalations (e.g. ACC-0012, ACC-0013, ACC-0015, ACC-0018, ACC-0020, ACC-0023, ACC-0026, AGY-0018, AGY-0020)
**Confidence:** MEDIUM (account manager and agency notes, second-hand; no travel manager wrote these words directly)
**Role:** Buyer (judges value and renewal); his travellers, like [[mei]], are the end users

## Identity
- **Age:** 50-60
- **Location:** Headquarters of a pharmaceutical company
- **Occupation:** Global travel manager
- **Bio:** Runs travel policy, the travel management company contract, and duty of care for about 3,000 travellers. Renews the airline contract every year and is preparing a tender. Gets calls from the CEO's office when travellers are stranded.

## Goals & Motivations
- **Primary Goal:** Know where travellers are during a disruption and have them rebooked fast, without his desk calling the airline one traveller at a time.
- **Secondary Goals:** Meet duty-of-care obligations; keep disruption costs (missed meetings, out-of-policy tickets) down; give the board a written plan.
- **Definition of Success:** Average time to rebook falls from over two hours to minutes; fewer escalations from executives.
- **Deeper Motivation:** Being able to defend his airline choice.

## Pain Points
- **Challenges:** 61 disrupted trips in a summer with average rebooking over two hours; the corporate line is overloaded while the app tells travellers to call; no status feed.
- **Blockers:** No self-service rebooking for travellers; no bulk tool or feed for the travel desk.
- **Workarounds:** Travel desk calls the corporate line per traveller; travellers buy other airlines and claim it back.
- **Emotional Impact:** Under pressure from leadership; considering moving routes.

## Behavior
- **Current Solutions:** Travel management company, account manager, monthly disruption reports.
- **Tools:** Travel management platform, spreadsheets, email
- **Frequency:** Weekly reviews; daily during disruption peaks
- **Tech Affinity:** Medium
- **Decision Style:** Data-driven, consensus with procurement

## Context & Environment
- **Usage Context:** Office, quarterly reviews, renewals and tenders.
- **Devices:** Laptop
- **Time Pressure:** Moderate, with spikes on disruption days.
- **Influencers:** CFO, CEO office, duty-of-care team, procurement.

## Scenario
**The renewal review**
Before his tender in February he asks the account manager for a written plan for disruption. He compares airlines on two numbers: how fast travellers get rebooked, and whether he can see it. A booking redesign does not interest him; self-service rebooking that his travellers actually use does.

## Communication Style
- **Tone:** Formal, diplomatic, firm
- **Speech Patterns:** Numbers, policy, "duty of care", "renewal"
- **Frustration Signals:** Flags the account at risk; trials a competitor on routes
- **Enthusiasm Signals:** Praises punctuality and the lounges in reviews
