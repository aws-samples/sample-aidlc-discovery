# Framing rules

Use these in the first two Working Backwards questions, Listen and Define, so the problem is framed sharply before anyone writes a press release. Write the results into the Working Backwards document.

## The end customer

Before framing, name the **end customer**: the person whose day changes when this ships. Not the team in the room, not the people who build it, not the sponsor. Ask: "If this ships perfectly, who has a better day tomorrow?" If the signals point to more than one, name the primary one and say who else is affected.

## Root causes: why, not what

Each root cause explains **why** the persona has the problem, as a structural condition, in the third person. "Repeat orders take 11.5 minutes" is a symptom. "The app does not keep a customer's last order, so every order starts from an empty basket" is a cause. For each bullet, ask: does this explain why? If it describes what happens, it is a symptom or an impact.

## Impact: customer and business, separately

Two short lists:
- **Impact on the customer:** what the persona loses: time, money, sleep, trust, opportunities.
- **Impact on the business:** what the company loses: revenue, retention, cost, reputation.

If every bullet is about the business, the customer's side is missing, and the other way round.

## Today statement

> Today, [persona] have to [problem] when [context]. They need a way to [outcome].

The "need a way to" clause describes an **outcome**, never a mechanism. Scan it for technology, features, or how it works: "AI-powered", "real-time", "dashboard that shows", "platform that", "automatically". If you find any, ask what outcome the mechanism gives the persona, and write that instead.

## How Might We

> How might we [outcome from the Today statement] for [persona] so that [benefit]?

One question, broad enough for several solutions, narrow enough to act on.

## North Star

> Deploy [capability] by [when] to [metric 1] and [metric 2], in order to [ultimate goal].

- The capability is a description, not a product name: "a way to repeat an order in under two minutes", not "Your usual". Product names belong in the PR/FAQ.
- The metrics are specific numbers tied to the business metric agreed at setup.
- The goal describes a positive end state, not the removal of a problem.
- These numbers are the workshop's targets. The PR/FAQ, the PRD, and the success criteria in Validate reuse them, or say why one differs.
