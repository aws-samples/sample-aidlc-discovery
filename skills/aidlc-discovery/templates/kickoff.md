Fill this in before the Discovery workshop and save it as `discovery/kickoff.md` in the working folder. At setup, the assistant takes the product, the team, the business metric, the candidate use cases, and the data from this page, so the day starts with one confirmation instead of rounds of questions. Write what the team believes: the data will test it. Leave out customer names, account names, and revenue amounts.
