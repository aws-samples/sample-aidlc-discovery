# Signal Brief template

```markdown
# Signal Brief: [theme in a few words]
Brief ID: SB-[number] | Written: [date] | Period covered: [from] to [to]

**Theme:** [the problem in one sentence]

**Job or desired outcome:** [what the customer is trying to achieve]

**Evidence:**
- "[anonymized quote]" ([source type, such as support ticket])
- "[anonymized quote]" ([source type])
- [volume: for example, about 35% of setup tickets in the last 90 days]

**Estimated impact:** [time, money, risk, or retention, even approximate; say if it is an estimate]

**Confidence:** [High | Medium | Low], because [the reason, such as the same pattern in three sources]

**Segment:** [who is affected, without personal data, such as administrators at companies with 50 to 500 employees]

**Suggested next step:** [what to test or learn]
```

## Example

```markdown
# Signal Brief: permission setup
Brief ID: SB-01 | Written: 2026-10-20 | Period covered: 2026-07-20 to 2026-10-20

**Theme:** Account administrators cannot finish permission setup without help.

**Job or desired outcome:** Give new users the right access on their first day.

**Evidence:**
- "I opened a ticket for every role I set up." (support ticket)
- "We gave everyone admin rights because the roles were too confusing." (onboarding call notes)
- About 35% of setup tickets in the last 90 days

**Estimated impact:** 2 to 3 days to onboard a team; a setup ticket in most new accounts (estimate)

**Confidence:** High, because the same pattern appears in tickets, onboarding calls, and churn notes

**Segment:** Administrators at companies with 50 to 500 employees

**Suggested next step:** Check whether the pain is the number of screens or unclear role names
```
